T1318550 — Recurrence editor labels with form.labelMode (v25.1.10 CDN)

Repro: Click any recurring appointment ("Weekly sync", "Daily standup") → "Open" → expand Repeat section. With labelMode='static' or 'floating', the inner editors of the recurrence form (e.g. byMonthDay, byDay) render their dataField names as labels. label.visible: false in m_recurrence_editor.ts only suppresses the outer Form labels.
