T1330691 - Scheduler: Popup Animation After Slot Selection

Steps to reproduce:

  1. Double-click on an empty time slot — popup opens with animation
  2. Close the popup (Cancel or Escape)
  3. Quickly double-click on the same or another slot — popup should open with animation again

Bug: On the CDN version (unfixed), the popup opens without animation on quick reopen.
Root cause: ResizeObserver callback fires during show animation and calls _stopAnimation().
