#1699 — Can resize out of group by one cell (horizontal grouping)

Resize · horizontal grouping · theme: Fluent Blue Light · DevExtreme 26.1

How to reproduce

  1. Grab the right edge of an appointment that sits at the boundary of its resource group.
  2. Drag it toward the neighbouring group.
Expected: You cannot resize an appointment outside of its own group.
Actual: It can be resized one cell into the neighbouring group (ahead or behind). Reproducible on any view.
PM question already on the issue: behaviour is inconsistent between horizontal and vertical grouping — needs a product ruling.
