#1699 — Can resize out of group by one cell (horizontal grouping)

Resize · horizontal grouping · theme: Fluent Blue Light · DevExtreme 26.1

How to reproduce

  1. Stay on the 'Horizontal Grouping' month view (two-month interval, groups side by side).
  2. Grab the left OR right edge of an appointment that sits at the boundary column of its priority group.
  3. Drag it across the boundary toward the neighbouring group.
Expected: You cannot resize an appointment outside of its own group.
Actual: It can be resized one cell into the neighbouring group (ahead or behind).
PM question already on the issue: behaviour is inconsistent between horizontal and vertical grouping — needs a product ruling.
