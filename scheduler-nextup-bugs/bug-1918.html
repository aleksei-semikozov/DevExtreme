#1918 — Broken resize step in vertical grouping (Material / Fluent only)

Resize · vertical grouping · theme: Fluent Blue Light · DevExtreme 26.1

How to reproduce

  1. Grab the bottom edge of an appointment.
  2. Drag slowly downward and watch how the end time snaps.
Expected: The resize step is exactly 30 minutes per cell.
Actual: The step is NOT 30 minutes. Only reproducible in Material/Fluent themes, where the all-day panel height differs from the regular cell height.
Mechanism: the vertical resize delta is computed as deltaHeightPx × cellDuration ÷ cellHeight (get_delta_time.ts). When the height passed in doesn't match the real cell height, the step drifts off 30 min.
